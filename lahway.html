Team project / Travel planning / Node.js & MySQL

LahWay. Building together under a tight deadline.

A collaborative travel and experience planning web application that brings together discovery, itineraries, bookings, reviews and expense tracking. My focus was the expense-management experience and selected interface pages.

Travel and experience planner homepage

The problem

Travellers often switch between different tools to discover activities, plan itineraries, track spending and manage bookings. LahWay was our team's attempt to bring those activities together in one platform.

My contributions

Expense management

Developed create, read, update and delete operations for expenses, with search and filtering by category and date, plus analytics for spending insights.

Interface and branding

Designed the landing page and About page and created a favicon, with an emphasis on a clean, understandable experience.

Database integration

Used SQL INNER JOIN queries to display meaningful trip and category names instead of database IDs.

Access and ownership

Used session-based authentication and ownership validation so users could view and change their own expense records.

Our biggest challenge

Our team had approximately one to two weeks to build the application. We worked on a shared Node.js application centred on app.js, and a conflicting change could stop the whole site from running. We held regular discussions to divide responsibilities, coordinate GitHub pushes and pulls, and resolve integration problems.

What I learned

This project taught me that development is not just about finishing my own feature. It also requires clear communication, version control, understanding how modules interact and checking that the whole application still works after changes are merged.

Prototype limitations

Our presentation identified opportunities for better booking automation, stronger production security, more scalable data views and additional mobile testing. LahWay should be understood as an academic prototype rather than a finished commercial booking service.
